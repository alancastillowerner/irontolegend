Iron to Legend: League of Legends career simulator

Iron to Legend is a free, unofficial League of Legends career simulator you play in your browser, with no account needed. Live a whole pro career in a few minutes: make the calls, play the key moments through skill minigames and share your story.

  • Build Your Legend: take a pro player from SoloQ to Worlds. Pick a country, role and main champion, sign with real teams, earn salaries, survive meta-changing patches and fight for titles until you retire.
  • Build Your Champion: design a champion and, as part of the Riot team, guide it through ten seasons of nerfs, buffs, reworks, skins and community drama.
  • Daily challenge: everyone starts with the same player and the same luck. Your first run of the day goes on the global ranking.
